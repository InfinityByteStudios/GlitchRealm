GlitchRealm Guides

Straight answers about playing, setting up and shipping browser games

Every game on GlitchRealm runs in the browser. That keeps things simple, but it also means the experience depends on your browser, your input device and your connection. These guides cover the questions we get most often from players and developers, written by the people who maintain the platform.

Start with the guide that matches your problem. Each one is a standalone article: you do not need to read them in order.

Where to go next
