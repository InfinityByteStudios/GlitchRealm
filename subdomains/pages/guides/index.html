GlitchRealm Guides

Straight answers about playing, setting up and shipping browser games

Every game on GlitchRealm runs in the browser. That keeps things simple, but it also means the experience depends on your browser, your input device and your connection. These guides cover the questions we get most often from players and developers, written by the people who maintain the platform.

Start with the guide that matches your problem. Each one is a standalone article: you do not need to read them in order.

Player guides

How to play browser games

The basics of launching a game, understanding controls, using fullscreen, keeping progress and fixing the most common startup problems. Read this first if you are new to browser gaming or if a game will not start.

Controller and keyboard setup

How browser games read input, which controllers work without extra software, how to connect a gamepad over Bluetooth and what to do when a key or button does nothing.

Performance on low-end devices

Practical settings for older laptops, budget phones and slow connections: browser hardware acceleration, tab management, resolution, extensions and network advice.

Developer guides

How to submit a game to GlitchRealm

What we look for in a submission, what to prepare before you apply, how review works and what happens after a game is published. This is the guide to read before filling in the submission form.

Where to go next

  • Browse the games library and pick something to play.
  • Visit the help centre if a guide did not solve your problem.
  • Contact the team for account, bug or partnership questions.
  • Open the submission form when your game is ready.

These guides are updated when the platform changes. If something here is out of date, tell us through the contact form and we will fix it.
